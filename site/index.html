ResearchJun–Aug 2026 · NSF-funded REU

PolyTSL

Text-conditioned polymer generation with a frozen encoder and generator.

I developed a 593K-parameter adapter between Text+ChemT5 and MolGPT, curated 13,020 text–structure pairs, and evaluated 89 graph-disjoint targets across five seeds.

The evaluation includes a parameter-matched MLP, retrieval baselines, and shuffled-text controls. The central question is whether the model actually uses its text input.

Research notes →Try the split experiment →

First-author manuscript draft. Results support text-sensitive conditioning; they do not establish an advantage over the MLP or exact structural recovery. Research assets remain private.

VectorTile

Exact top-k search without the full distance matrix.

A C++17/Python lab comparing a bounded heap with tiled NumPy search. It covers deterministic ties, query-level threading, numeric edge cases, and a checked C interface.
