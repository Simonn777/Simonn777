VectorTile

Every vector.
Less distance workspace.

How do you find the exact nearest neighbors without storing every query-to-corpus distance at once?

This C++17/Python lab compares streaming heaps with tiled NumPy search. It makes parallelism, deterministic ties and numerical edge cases visible.

02 / Machine-learning researchJun–Aug 2026 · NSF-funded REU

PolyTSL

A small adapter.
A testable research question.

Can a compact trainable bridge make a frozen polymer generator respond to text?

I developed the adapter, curated 13,020 text–structure pairs across 735 polymer graphs, and evaluated 89 graph-disjoint targets across five seeds. The study includes MLP and retrieval baselines, plus shuffled-text ablations.

What the results support — and what they leave open +

The controlled experiments support text-sensitive conditioning relative to no-text and perturbation controls. They do not establish a significant advantage over the pooled MLP, exact repeat-unit recovery, or state-of-the-art performance.

First-author manuscript draft. Research source, data and model assets remain private pending release review; the linked page is a public methods summary.
